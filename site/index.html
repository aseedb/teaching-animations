Teaching animations

Interactive animations for histology and cell biology, made at the Institute of Anatomy of the University of Bern. They run in any browser, on a laptop, a tablet or a phone, in German, French and English.

  • Aktin am Lamellipodium

    L'actine dans le lamellipode · Actin at the lamellipodium

    Actin treadmilling at the leading edge of a crawling cell: ATP, ADP-Pi and ADP actin, Arp2/3 branches, capping protein, cofilin. Guided tour in nine steps.

    DeutschFrançaisEnglish

  • Dynamische Instabilität

    Instabilité dynamique · Dynamic instability

    A microtubule grows and shrinks: GTP cap, catastrophe, rescue, the cell edge, and what the tubulin concentration changes. Guided tour in nine steps.

    DeutschFrançaisEnglish

  • Sarkomer und Kraft

    Sarcomère et force · Sarcomere and force

    How the A band, I band and H zone change with sarcomere length, and why force depends on the overlap of actin and myosin. Rabbit and frog data, optional titin.

    DeutschFrançaisEnglish

  • Desmosomen unter Zug

    Desmosomes sous traction · Desmosomes under tension

    Two epithelia under the same pulling force, with and without keratin filaments anchoring their desmosomes; a link to epidermolysis bullosa simplex.

    DeutschFrançaisEnglish
